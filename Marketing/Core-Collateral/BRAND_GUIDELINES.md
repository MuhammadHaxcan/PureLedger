## 3. Logo Usage

### Primary Logo
The PureLedger logo consists of the wordmark "PureLedger" with an integrated ledger book icon.

### Logo Variations
1. **Full Logo** - Wordmark + Icon (preferred)
2. **Icon Only** - For small applications (min 32x32px)
3. **Wordmark Only** - When horizontal space is limited

### Clear Space
Maintain minimum clear space equal to the height of the letter "P" on all sides.

### Minimum Sizes
- Digital: 120px wide (full logo)
- Print: 1 inch wide (full logo)
- Icon only: 32px / 0.25 inch

### Incorrect Usage
- Do not rotate the logo
- Do not stretch or distort
- Do not change colors outside brand palette
- Do not add effects (shadows, gradients, outlines)
- Do not place on busy backgrounds

## 6. Voice & Tone

### Brand Voice
- **Professional** but approachable
- **Confident** without being arrogant
- **Clear** and jargon-free
- **Supportive** and solution-oriented

### Tone Guidelines

| Context | Tone | Example |
|---------|------|---------|
| Marketing | Inspiring, benefit-focused | "Transform your trading operations with precision accounting" |
| Technical | Clear, instructive | "To create a delivery challan, navigate to DC Processing" |
| Error messages | Helpful, non-blaming | "We couldn't find that voucher. Check the ID and try again" |
| Success | Celebratory, brief | "Voucher created successfully!" |

### Writing Principles

1. **Lead with benefits, follow with features**
   - Yes: "Save hours on reconciliation with automated DC matching"
   - No: "Our system has automated DC matching features"

2. **Use active voice**
   - Yes: "PureLedger tracks every delivery"
   - No: "Every delivery is tracked by PureLedger"

3. **Be specific with numbers**
   - Yes: "Reduce reconciliation time by 60%"
   - No: "Significantly reduce reconciliation time"

4. **Address the reader directly**
   - Yes: "You can track deliveries in real-time"
   - No: "Users can track deliveries in real-time"

## 8. Visual Style

### Photography Style
- **Subject:** Real business environments (warehouses, offices, delivery operations)
- **Style:** Natural lighting, candid moments, diverse representation
- **Mood:** Professional, trustworthy, modern

### Iconography
- Style: Outlined, 2px stroke weight
- Corner radius: 2px
- Size: 24x24px standard
- Color: Single color from brand palette

### Data Visualization
- Use brand colors consistently
- Debit = Alert Red (#EF4444)
- Credit = Success Green (#10B981)
- Neutral = PureLedger Blue (#2563EB)

### UI Screenshots
When capturing product screenshots:
- Use sample data that looks realistic
- Blur or mask any sensitive information
- Capture at high resolution (2x)
- Maintain consistent window sizing

### Social Media

- Tone: Professional, thought leadership

**Facebook:**
- Profile: Icon only
- Cover: Feature highlight + benefit
- Tone: Engaging, community-focused

- Profile: Icon only

- Title slide: Logo centered, tagline below
- Content slides: Logo in bottom right corner
- Use brand colors for charts and highlights
- Include page numbers

### Print Materials
- Brochures: Full bleed, high-quality paper (minimum 100gsm)
- Business cards: 350gsm, matte finish
- One-pagers: Single or double-sided, A4/Letter size
