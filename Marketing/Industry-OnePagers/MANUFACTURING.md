## How PureLedger Solves These

### Recipe System for BOM
Create transaction templates that mirror your bills of materials.

```
Recipe: "Steel Frame Assembly"
├── Debit: Raw Material A (qty: 4)
├── Debit: Raw Material B (qty: 2)
├── Credit: Finished Good X (qty: 1)
└── Saved for one-click voucher creation
```

- Save complex vouchers as templates
- Consistent entries every production run
- Modify quantities as needed
- Reduce data entry errors

### Product Quantity Tracking
Track both quantities and values for all inventory items.

| Feature | Capability |
|---------|------------|
| Debit entries | Quantity increases (purchases, production output) |
| Credit entries | Quantity decreases (sales, production input) |
| Running balance | Real-time quantity position |
| Average costing | Automatic average rate calculation |

### Multi-Category Organization
Organize accounts by product line, material type, or process stage.

```
Project: Main Factory
├── Category: Raw Materials
│   ├── Steel
│   ├── Aluminum
│   └── Plastics
├── Category: WIP
│   └── Semi-finished items
├── Category: Finished Goods
│   ├── Product Line A
│   └── Product Line B
└── Category: Customers
```

### Delivery Challan for Shipments
Track outbound shipments of finished goods.

- Process DCs for customer deliveries
- Multiple delivery locations
- Partial shipment support
- FOR and cartage for shipping costs

### Product Profitability Reports
Understand true margin by product.

| Metric | Calculation |
|--------|-------------|
| Total Cost | Average rate × quantity sold |
| Revenue | Actual selling value |
| Margin | Revenue - Cost |
| Margin % | (Margin / Revenue) × 100 |

## Manufacturing Workflow Example

```
1. Raw Material Purchase
   └── Voucher: Debit Steel (qty: 100kg), Credit Supplier

2. Production Run (using Recipe)
   └── Voucher: Debit Finished Product (qty: 20), Credit Steel (qty: 50kg)

3. Customer Sale
   └── Voucher: Debit Customer, Credit Finished Product (qty: 15)

4. Delivery Processing
   └── DC created, shipment tracked, FOR allocated

5. Reporting
   └── Product report shows COGS, margin per product
```
