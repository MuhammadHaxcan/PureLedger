## How PureLedger Solves These

### Native DC Integration
Create sale vouchers and delivery challans together - not as separate systems that need reconciliation.

```
Sale Voucher Created → Products Selected for DC →
DC Processed → Entries Marked Complete →
No Monthly Reconciliation Needed
```

### Real-Time Credit Management
Customer balance updates instantly when transactions are entered - not at month-end.

- See current balance before approving new orders
- Aging report shows exactly who owes what
- Creditable date tracking for accurate payment terms

### Automatic Freight Allocation
FOR charges and cartage costs flow directly to the right accounts.

| Charge Type | Automation |
|-------------|------------|
| FOR Charges | Auto-posted to customer or expense account |
| Cartage | Auto-linked to transporter and customer |
| Builty Tracking | Reference number captured on DC |

### Partial Delivery Tracking
Base voucher system tracks what's been shipped vs. pending.

- Create base voucher for full order
- Ship partial quantities, create derived vouchers
- See remaining quantity at a glance
- Complete tracking of multi-delivery orders

### Location-Based Operations
Track deliveries to different locations (addas, stations, depots).

- Define locations in system
- Assign location to each DC
- Filter reports by location
- Understand which locations have what inventory
