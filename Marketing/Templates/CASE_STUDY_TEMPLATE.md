## Writing Guidelines

### Voice & Tone
- Customer-centric: They are the hero, PureLedger is the tool
- Data-driven: Include specific numbers wherever possible
- Authentic: Use real quotes, don't make them too polished
- Professional: Avoid hype, let results speak

### Quote Collection Tips
- Interview customer for 30-45 minutes
- Ask open-ended questions: "How did that change?"
- Listen for colorful language and specific examples
- Get approval on final quotes before publishing

### Numbers to Capture
- Before/after metrics (time, errors, costs)
- Implementation timeline
- Number of users
- Transaction volumes
- Any financial impact (savings, recovered revenue)

### Photo Requirements
- Customer headshot (with permission)
- Company logo (get proper file)
- Optional: Office/warehouse photos (if relevant)

### Approval Process
1. Draft case study
2. Internal review
3. Send to customer for approval
4. Get signed release form
5. Finalize and publish

## Sample Interview Questions

### About Their Challenge
1. Before PureLedger, what system were you using?
2. What was your biggest frustration with that approach?
3. Can you walk me through your typical month-end close process then?
4. How much time did DC reconciliation take?
5. What was the impact of freight misallocation or errors?

### About the Selection
1. What made you start looking for a new solution?
2. What alternatives did you consider?
3. What made PureLedger stand out?
4. Were there any concerns during evaluation?

### About Implementation
1. How long did implementation take?
2. How was the data migration?
3. How did your team adapt to the new system?
4. What was the most challenging part?

### About Results
1. What's different now compared to before?
2. What specific time savings have you seen?
3. Have errors decreased? By how much?
4. Any financial impact you can quantify?
5. What's been the biggest surprise benefit?

### Closing
1. Would you recommend PureLedger to others? Why?
2. What advice would you give to someone considering PureLedger?
3. What's next for your use of the system?
