## Frequently Asked Questions

### Billing

**When am I charged?**
Monthly plans are charged on the same date each month. Annual plans are charged upfront.

**Can I change plans?**
Yes, you can upgrade anytime (prorated). Downgrades take effect at the next billing cycle.

**What payment methods do you accept?**
Credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex), bank transfers (for annual plans), and regional payment methods where available.

**Is there a free trial?**
Yes, 14-day free trial with full Professional features, no credit card required.

### Regional Pricing

**How do I qualify for regional pricing?**
Regional pricing is available for businesses headquartered in qualifying countries. We verify based on billing address and primary operations.

**Can I pay in local currency?**
Yes, in supported regions. Otherwise, USD is accepted universally.

### Contracts

**Is there a contract?**
Monthly plans have no long-term contract. Annual plans require 12-month commitment.

**What's the cancellation policy?**
Monthly: Cancel anytime, access continues through current period.
Annual: Non-refundable, but can downgrade at renewal.

### Limits

**What happens if I exceed user limits?**
You'll be notified and asked to add users or upgrade. We won't cut off access without warning.

**What happens if I exceed project limits?**
Same as users - you'll need to upgrade or purchase additional projects.
